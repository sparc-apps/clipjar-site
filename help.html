How items get into the history

Mac

ClipJar watches the clipboard: everything you copy (⌘C) appears in the history by itself. Copies from excluded apps (password managers by default) are ignored. Menu bar icon → Pause history: while it's on, what you copy isn't saved (the clipboard itself works as usual).

iPhone and iPad

iOS does not let apps watch the clipboard. Add items with the Paste button at the top, with Share → ClipJar, with the ClipJar keyboard or with a Shortcuts automation. The Pause button (turn it on in Appearance) stops checking the clipboard when ClipJar opens; Paste and Share still work.

Pasting

Mac

Press ⌥⌘V (you can change it in Settings) to open the panel. ↑↓ choose an item, Return pastes it, ⇧Return pastes plain text, ⌘1…⌘9 paste by number. ←→ collapse and expand a group (or a list of files); ⌥↑⌥↓ move an item within its group. The panel opens next to the text cursor where you type; if the app doesn't report the cursor, next to the mouse pointer.

iPhone and iPad

Tap an item to copy it, then paste in any app as usual. Long-press an item for more: copy as plain text, actions, move, delete.

Profiles

Mac

A profile is a set of features: what ClipJar recognizes and which actions it offers. Simple is just copy and paste, Everyday adds screenshots and text from images, and the other profiles are for development, finance and content work. Choose one in Settings → Features or from the menu bar icon → Profile. Mac profiles are separate from iPhone and iPad profiles. Your own profiles (Pro) sync to your other Macs; the chosen profile stays separate on each Mac.

iPhone and iPad

A profile decides which buttons and menu items you see. Simple is a plain list: tap an item to copy it. Standard and Full add pin, move, edit, filters, actions and labels. Choose one in Settings → Appearance → Profile. iPhone and iPad profiles are separate from Mac profiles. Your own profiles (Pro) sync to your other iPhones and iPads; the chosen profile stays separate on each device.

Pin, move, delete

MaciPhone and iPad

Pinned items stay at the top and are never removed by the history limit or retention. Arrows move an item within its group. A deleted item can be restored with Undo (⌘Z on Mac). A group of items moves as a whole; the order is kept after a restart. On Mac, ⌥⌘↑ moves an item or a group to the top. With Pro sync on, the order of pinned items is the same on all your devices.

Labels, trash and multi-select

Mac

Right-click an item → Label to give it a color (🔴🟠🟡🟢🔵🟣⚪️). Labels sync to your other devices. Deleted items go to the trash (🗑 in the header): restore, preview or delete forever. ⌘Z undoes the last deletion. In Pro the trash can be turned off in History settings. ⌘-click marks several items; right-click → Select all / Delete (N). ⌘⌫ deletes the marked items. Esc closes the window.

iPhone and iPad

Long-press an item → Label to give it a color. Labels sync to your other devices. Deleted items go to the trash (🗑 next to Edit): swipe to restore or delete forever, long-press for preview. In Pro the trash can be turned off in Settings. Tap Edit, mark items with the circles, then Select all / Delete (N) at the bottom. Settings → Appearance → Profile → Simple hides everything extra.

Search and filters

MaciPhone and iPad

Type to search the whole history. Filters show only links, code, tables, images, IBANs and other types.

Groups and filter by app

MaciPhone and iPad

The ⧉ button groups pinned and recent items by app, device or type: 2 or more items become one row with a count. Click or tap it to expand; ← collapses it. Right-click or swipe a group to pin, unpin or delete all of it (deleted items go to Recently deleted). The App filter shows items from one app only. Groups are hidden while you search or filter.

Cards view

Mac

Settings → Appearance → Interface → Cards (Pro): a compact window with items as cards. Click a card to paste it. The ⋯ button or a right-click lets you pin, preview, paste as plain text, recognize text or delete. The ⋯ at the top sets pasting without formatting and what cards show. Sync, protection, screenshots and isolated clipboards keep working; profiles and other features apply in the Standard interface. You can switch back any time.

Recently deleted

MaciPhone and iPad

Deleted items go to Recently deleted (Pro), where you can restore them or delete them for good. A group deleted as a whole stays one row: restore or delete it at once, or expand it to handle items one by one. Codes and card numbers leave the trash when they expire. The trash lives in memory only and is emptied when ClipJar quits; in the Cards view, only the last deletion is kept for Undo.

Smart actions

MaciPhone and iPad

ClipJar recognizes what you copied and offers fitting actions: format JSON, clean a link, fix text typed in the wrong layout, check an IBAN, split requisites into fields. The result becomes a new item.

Open and edit

Mac

Right-click → Open / Edit… or press ⌘O. Text opens in an editor (⌘S saves), images in a zoomable preview, files in their default app. Tables and formatted text keep their original styles (colors, fonts, merged cells) on a white page; the palette button switches to theme colors.

iPhone and iPad

Tap the pencil button in a row, or touch and hold → Open / Edit. Formatted text keeps bold and italic. Tables and formatted text keep their original styles (colors, fonts, merged cells) on a white page; the palette button switches to theme colors.

Open in… and Markup

Mac

Right-click an item → Open in… to edit a copy in another app (TextEdit, Preview, your code editor). Save it there with ⌘S and the change comes back to ClipJar. Pro.

iPhone and iPad

Touch and hold an image → Markup to draw on it, add text or crop it. Tap Done and the edited image replaces the item in the same place. Pro.

ClipJar keyboard

iPhone and iPad

Paste from the history in any app: Settings → General → Keyboard → Keyboards → Add New Keyboard → ClipJar, allow Full Access, then turn on access in ClipJar settings. Switch keyboards with 🌐. It shows text only, never codes or cards.

Shortcuts automation

iPhone and iPad

The “Add to ClipJar” action saves the clipboard without opening the app. Put it on Back Tap, the Action button or a personal automation.

Privacy

Mac

ClipJar never uses the network. History on disk is encrypted, the key is kept in Keychain. One-time codes, card numbers and personal IDs are hidden and deleted soon. Copies from excluded apps are ignored.

iPhone and iPad

ClipJar never uses the network. History on the device is encrypted. One-time codes, card numbers and personal IDs are hidden and deleted soon. Universal Clipboard stays off unless you allow it. Turn on clearing on screen lock under Privacy and unpinned items are removed when the iPhone locks.

Hidden codes and cards

MaciPhone and iPad

One-time codes, card numbers and personal IDs are hidden behind dots and deleted automatically. To see one, choose Show in its menu. If ClipJar hid something that isn’t secret (an order number, for example), choose Not a secret and the item becomes a normal one.

Isolated clipboards

Mac

Pro: Settings → Privacy → Isolated clipboards. Each listed app (for example Terminal) gets its own clipboard: switch to it and its clipboard comes back, switch away and your shared clipboard returns. Copies there aren’t saved to history unless you allow it, and are synced only with a second permission. Isolated clipboards never go to your iPhone through Apple’s Universal Clipboard; text copied on the iPhone while such an app is open goes to the shared clipboard. The feature must be on in your profile (Features). With Pro sync on, this list and the “Don't record from these apps” list are shared by all your Macs. An app that isn’t installed on one of them is shown there as “Not installed on this Mac”.

iPhone and iPad

Isolated clipboards are available on Mac only: iOS doesn’t tell apps which other app is open, so a separate clipboard per app isn’t possible on iPhone and iPad. Items copied in isolated apps on your Mac reach the iPhone only if you allow syncing them on the Mac.

How long items are kept

MaciPhone and iPad

History depth limits how many items are kept; retention removes unpinned items after the time you choose. Clearing the history can be undone right after. Items larger than the size limits (images and text, in History settings) aren’t added; formatting that’s too large is kept as plain text.

Sync between your devices

MaciPhone and iPad

Pro: turn on Settings → Sync → Shared pinned items and choose the same iCloud Drive folder and the same password on every device. Pinned items, labels and your own profiles are stored there encrypted; ClipJar itself never goes online. My devices lists every device: you can stop accepting changes from one or forget an old one. An item you delete is deleted everywhere. Your own formats and languages sync too. To add a device later, turn on sync there with the same folder and password. A device that hasn't synced for a long time is forgotten automatically (Settings → Sync → Forget silent devices after, 1 year by default); if it comes back, items you deleted elsewhere stay only on it. If you forget the password, the synced copies can't be decrypted, but your local history stays. Password: at least 8 characters, no spaces at the start or end. Latin letters are easiest to type on every device.

Your own languages and formats

MaciPhone and iPad

Language: Settings → Advanced → Create template, translate the lines (the help.* lines translate this Help too), set your own code and name, press Reload and choose it in Appearance → Language. Number format: Create template under Recognized numbers, edit it and press Reload; What’s recognized lists everything ClipJar knows. With sync on, your languages and formats reach your other devices.

Moving to another device

MaciPhone and iPad

Export your settings to a file (optionally with a password) and import it on another Mac or iPhone. Both use the same file. Options the other device doesn't have are kept for the way back.

Reset settings

MaciPhone and iPad

Reset all settings (Settings → Advanced) brings back the defaults. History, pinned items, your purchase, sync and your own profiles are kept, and Restore previous settings brings your old settings back.

Free and Pro

MaciPhone and iPad

Free: 5 history items, 3 pinned items, two basic profiles, basic actions and full protection of codes and cards. Pro: up to 1000 items, unlimited pins, all profiles and your own, smart actions, bank details, editing, sync between your devices, saved history and more.
