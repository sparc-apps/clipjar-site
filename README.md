# ClipJar

Clipboard history for Mac, iPhone and iPad — everything you copy, one keystroke away.

- **Website, guides and videos:** https://clipjar.sparcapps.dev
- **Mac App Store / App Store:** coming soon
- **Support:** support@sparcapps.dev

## What it does
- Press **⌥⌘V** on the Mac — your clipboard history opens right where you type. Search, Return to paste.
- Text, links, images, files, tables and formatted documents, with their original styles.
- Pins, labels, groups, smart actions, a compact Cards view.
- On iPhone and iPad — your history in the app and in the ClipJar keyboard.
- Optional encrypted sync through your own iCloud Drive folder.

## Privacy
ClipJar works fully offline: no account, no analytics, no tracking, no servers of its own.
Codes, card numbers and passwords are hidden and removed automatically.
Privacy policy: https://clipjar.sparcapps.dev/privacy.html
